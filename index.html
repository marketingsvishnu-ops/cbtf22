FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How can I stay motivated every day?

Focus on small actions instead of waiting for a burst of motivation. Create a routine and make progress easier to repeat.

What if I fail?

Failure is information, not a final destination. Learn what went wrong, adjust your approach and try again.

How big should my goals be?

Choose goals that challenge you while still being realistic. Then divide them into smaller milestones.

Do I need motivation before taking action?

No. Very often action creates motivation. Start with something small and let momentum build naturally.
